Six stages, in order

03 — How we work
01

Conduit in roof and floor

Pipes laid in the roof slab and floor to the drawing you provide, so the wiring can be pulled through afterwards.

02

Switchboard boxes

Metal boxes cut into the wall at every switchboard position, set to level and to height before plaster closes it.

03

Wiring pulled through

Separate circuits for light, fan, AC, camera and power points — loads split so no single line carries more than it should.

04

Distribution board

Every circuit terminated in the DB on its own MCB, and each MCB tagged and labelled — so a future fault is found in seconds.

05

Lights, fans and switches

Fitting starts after paint, so nothing finished gets damaged. Every point is run and tested in front of you.

06

Switches, your way

Plates, finish and switch layout customised to your preference, grouped the way the room is actually used.
